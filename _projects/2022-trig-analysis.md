Working with the Tompkins Consolidated Area Transit, my sub-team has been designing a sustainable bus shelter prototype for potential implementation.

Some key challenges we faced in this project included:
Creating something easily manufacturable and assemblable, reducing materials wasted during assembly.
Sourcing the correct materials to use to achieve carbon neutrality/ embodied carbon reduction
Scalable and Modular Design that can fit into smaller and larger spaces.


Ultimately, the team agreed to a design, using Fusion360 and Revit, involving a skeleton of multiple triangular modules, with a glass skin surrounding the body, protecting it from natural elements.
As of this semester, our Alpha Prototype consists of a CNC routing (right picture) manufactured prototype.
	
Moving forward, this semester’s work currently involves:
How to connect the glass skin to the module?
Embedding and insulating electrical components from heat extremities.
Exploring alternative module shapes, including trapezoidal forms, for potential efficiency improvements.
