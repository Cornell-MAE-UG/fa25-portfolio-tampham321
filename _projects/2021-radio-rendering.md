Working with 4-5 other colleagues, we developed a cost-effective backpack attachment to hold your skis. Over an entire semester, we have gone through the design, prototyping, and manufacturing process to build a final product, while maintaining a budget of $600.

Some key challenges we faced while designing this prototype:

The gear fabrication. To create the gears, we created CAD files and sent the order to the rapid prototyping lab with a laser cutter. Unfortunately, due to the sheer size and inaccuracies, transferring file types out of our hands resulted in misfitting gears and, therefore, a misfit final prototype.
The slider we used did not have a locking mechanism to adjust to the different distances between bindings. We did not take this into account when we ordered the part from McMaster. This resulted in custom 3D printed parts that drilled a hole into the sliders held by a bolt.
Other issues include manufacturing processes that include a lot of symmetry issues due to a lack of equipment.
